How to permanently delete your Paddock account and data

Paddock (developer: Paddock; app: com.paddockapp.paddock) lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Delete from within the app (fastest)

  1. Open Paddock and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account permanently.

Your account is deleted immediately. This action is irreversible.

Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address associated with your account:

Email: support@paddockapp.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request
Include your account email or username so we can verify and process the request.

We will verify and delete your data within 30 days of a verified request.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile (display name, bio, location, avatar).
  • Your vehicles, modifications, and service/maintenance records.
  • Your wishlist items.
  • Your follows, blocks, conversations, and messages.
  • Any reports you submitted.
  • Your uploaded images (avatar, vehicle, mod, and service photos).
  • Your authentication tokens and push-notification token.

What we keep

None of your personal data is retained after deletion. We may retain limited, aggregated, non-identifying diagnostic data (e.g. anonymous crash counts) that cannot be linked back to you.
