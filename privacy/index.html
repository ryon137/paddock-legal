PrancyCar

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

PrancyCar ("we", "our", or "us") is a vehicle garage tracking and enthusiast community app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and how you can delete it.

By creating an account or using PrancyCar, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Account details — email address and username (required to create an account). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google.
  • Profile information — display name, bio, location (optional), and any profile or avatar photo you choose to upload.
  • Garage content — vehicles, modifications, service/maintenance records, wishlist items, and any photos you attach to them.
  • Messages — direct messages you send to other users.
  • Reports — if you report or block another user, we record that action.

Information collected automatically:

  • Authentication tokens — to keep you signed in securely.
  • Device push token — a Firebase Cloud Messaging token, so we can deliver push notifications you have enabled.
  • Diagnostic data — crash reports and basic error diagnostics (via Firebase Crashlytics) to help us fix bugs. These do not include the contents of your garage or messages.

Camera & Photo Library

We request camera and photo library access only when you choose to upload a profile photo or a vehicle/mod/service photo. We do not access your camera or photos at any other time.

Receipt Scanning (on-device)

If you use receipt scanning to log service records, the image is processed entirely on your device using on-device text recognition. Receipt images are not uploaded to our servers for scanning.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the app and your account.
  • To sync your garage data across your devices.
  • To power community features you choose to use (profiles, following, feed, direct messages).
  • To send push notifications you have enabled.
  • To maintain safety (blocking, reporting, rate limiting, abuse prevention).
  • To diagnose crashes and improve reliability.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

How Your Data Is Shared

  • Other users — content you mark public (your public profile, public vehicles and their mods/service history) and messages you send are visible to the users you share them with. Private vehicles are visible only to you.
  • Service providers (processors) that operate our infrastructure on our behalf:
    • Google Cloud (Cloud Run) — application hosting.
    • Supabase — database and image storage (hosted on AWS, United States).
    • Google Firebase — push notifications (Cloud Messaging), remote configuration, and crash reporting (Crashlytics).
    • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Legal — we may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and service.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud and Supabase infrastructure located in the United States. Access is restricted, connections are encrypted in transit (TLS), and passwords are stored only as salted hashes — never in plain text.

Data Retention & Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account.

Deleting your account immediately and permanently removes your profile, vehicles, modifications, service records, wishlist, follows, blocks, conversations, messages, reports, and uploaded images. This action is irreversible.

You may also request deletion by email — see Delete Your Account. We will delete your data within 30 days of a verified request.

Children's Privacy

PrancyCar is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. You can exercise most of these rights directly in the app, or by contacting us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
